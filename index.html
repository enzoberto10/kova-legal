KOVA — Privacy Policy

Last updated / Dernière mise à jour : June 12, 2026

KOVA is an AI-powered fitness coaching app for strength and running. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

KOVA is operated by Enzo Bertolami (the "Developer"). Contact: enzo.bertolami1@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Profile you provide

  • First name (optional)
  • Age, sex, height, weight, weight unit
  • Training preferences: goal, level, days per week, session length, training time, sport focus (strength / running / both), available equipment, injuries
  • Running profile: target distance, current pace, weekly mileage, race date
  • Strength baselines: 1-rep-max for squat, bench, deadlift, overhead press
  • Nutrition preferences: diet type, allergies, caloric goal, cook time, grocery budget, custom macro targets
  • Coach preferences: coaching style, voice replies, language, notification settings

2.2 Apple HealthKit (only with your explicit consent)

If you enable HealthKit in Settings → Connections, KOVA reads the following from Apple Health to personalize coaching:

  • Sleep hours
  • Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
  • Resting Heart Rate
  • Steps and active energy (today)
  • Workouts and runs (recent history)
  • Body weight history
  • VO2 Max

This data stays on your device and is sent to our coach AI only as part of the coaching context. You can revoke HealthKit access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health.

2.3 Logs you create

  • Workout sessions (exercises, weights, reps, RPE, duration)
  • Runs (distance, pace, duration)
  • Food logs (meals, macros)
  • Body weight measurements
  • Personal records (PRs)
  • Conversations with the AI coach (text + transient voice recordings)

2.4 Voice (if you use the voice features)

When you hold the microphone button or use voice during a workout, a short audio recording is captured locally, then sent to our backend for transcription (OpenAI Whisper). The text is then processed by our coach AI (Anthropic Claude). The audio is not stored after transcription. Coach replies may be synthesized to audio via ElevenLabs and played back.

2.5 What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location tracking beyond what HealthKit provides if you enable it
  • No contacts, photos, calendar
  • No payment info (Premium is handled by Apple via the App Store)

3. How we use your data

  • Generate your personalized training program (weekly plan, daily session)
  • Provide AI coaching responses tailored to your data
  • Adapt training load based on recovery signals (sleep, HRV, RHR)
  • Calculate calorie and macro targets (TDEE)
  • Detect plateaus, suggest deloads, recognize PRs
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app stability (via Sentry, anonymized)

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Third-party processors

To deliver the service, we share minimal data with the following providers, each under their own privacy terms:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — coach text generation. Your messages, profile context, recent logs are sent for processing. anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • OpenAI (Whisper) — speech-to-text transcription. Audio is sent for processing. openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech synthesis. Reply text is sent for processing. elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy
  • Supabase — backend hosting (Edge Functions + database). Data is stored encrypted at rest. supabase.com/privacy
  • Sentry — crash reporting (anonymized device info, no profile data). sentry.io/privacy
  • Apple — App Store distribution, in-app purchases, optional HealthKit access. apple.com/legal/privacy

5. Storage and security

Your profile and logs are stored locally on your device (encrypted by iOS) and may be synced to our Supabase backend (EU region, encrypted at rest, HTTPS in transit). API keys are never exposed to the client.

6. Retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You can delete all data by uninstalling KOVA, or by emailing enzo.bertolami1@gmail.com and requesting deletion of any cloud-synced records.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email enzo.bertolami1@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

KOVA is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Third-party processors (Anthropic, OpenAI, ElevenLabs) may process data in the United States. By using KOVA, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: enzo.bertolami1@gmail.com

KOVA est éditée par Enzo Bertolami (le « Développeur »). Contact : enzo.bertolami1@gmail.com.

  • Préférences d'entraînement : objectif, niveau, jours par semaine, durée de séance, horaire, focus (force / course / les deux), équipement, blessures

Si tu actives HealthKit dans Réglages → Connexions, KOVA lit les données suivantes depuis Apple Santé pour personnaliser le coaching :

  • Heures de sommeil
  • Variabilité de la fréquence cardiaque (HRV)
  • Fréquence cardiaque au repos
  • Pas et énergie active (du jour)
  • Séances et runs (historique récent)

  • VO2 Max

2.3 Logs que tu crées

  • Séances de musculation (exos, charges, reps, RPE, durée)
  • Runs (distance, allure, durée)
  • Journal alimentaire (repas, macros)
  • Mesures de poids corporel

2.4 Voix (si tu utilises les fonctionnalités vocales)

Quand tu maintiens le bouton micro ou utilises la voix pendant une séance, un court enregistrement audio est capturé localement, puis envoyé à notre backend pour transcription (OpenAI Whisper). Le texte est ensuite traité par le coach IA (Anthropic Claude). L'audio n'est pas stocké après transcription. Les réponses du coach peuvent être synthétisées en audio via ElevenLabs et jouées.

2.5 Ce que nous NE collectons PAS

  • Pas de tracking de localisation au-delà de ce que HealthKit fournit si activé

3. Comment nous utilisons tes données

  • Générer ton programme d'entraînement personnalisé (plan hebdo, séance du jour)
  • Fournir des réponses de coaching adaptées à tes données
  • Adapter la charge selon les signaux de récup (sommeil, HRV, FC repos)
  • Calculer les objectifs caloriques et macros (TDEE)
  • Détecter les plateaux, suggérer des deloads, reconnaître les PR
  • Diagnostiquer les crashes et améliorer la stabilité de l'app (via Sentry, anonymisé)

Nous ne vendons pas tes données. Nous n'utilisons pas tes données à des fins publicitaires.

4. Sous-traitants tiers

Pour fournir le service, nous partageons un minimum de données avec les prestataires suivants, chacun sous ses propres conditions :

5. Stockage et sécurité

9. Transferts internationaux
